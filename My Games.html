Final Door


A 2D platformer game where the main objective is to survive obstacles, avoid enemies, collect coins, and reach the final door to complete the level. The game includes character movement, enemy interactions, coin collection mechanics, and level progression.

ENTOMOPHOBIA


A RPG Game Based on a Phobia from insects where the main character works to save a village from dangerous insects emerging from a mysterious cave. Along the journey,the character receives help from a trusted friend while fighting enemies, exploring areas, and protecting the villagers.

Roll A Ball


A 3D Maze Game inspired by the Unity Roll-a-Ball project, where the player controls a rolling ball and explores a custom-built maze to collect all the coins hidden throughout the corridors. The game focuses on navigation, timing, and exploration while challenging the player to find every collectible before reaching victory.
